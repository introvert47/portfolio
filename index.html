About

I started by copying code from YouTube tutorials around when ChatGPT launched, then kept pulling things apart until I understood them well enough to write my own. No bootcamp, no CS classes yet — just a lot of broken builds and late nights fixing them, including picking up LLM fine-tuning along the way.

I'd rather ship something useful than something that only looks good in a demo: a tool that actually speeds up a slow laptop, an assistant that remembers what you told it last week, a chatbot people come back to daily. If it doesn't hold up outside the pitch, it's not finished.
